# Accessories — What Each One Is FOR

> Education + product-detail copy. Accessories are impulse/add-on buys: low price, high margin, easy "while you're here." The job of the copy is to answer "what does this do and do I need it?" in one or two sentences.

Group these on the site by *job*: **Grip & Hand**, **Tape & Skin**, **Ball Care**, **Carry & Storage**, **Support & Protection**.

---

## Grip & Hand

### Rosin bag / grip sack
- **What it is:** a small porous bag of rosin or powder you squeeze to coat your gripping hand.
- **What it's for:** **sweaty or slick hands.** Adds tackiness so the ball doesn't slip early off your thumb. Cheap, league-legal in most play.
- **Buy it if:** your hands sweat, or the ball ever feels like it's "falling off" your hand.

### Grip / dry-hand cream and "easy slide" products
- **What they're for:** the flip side of rosin — some bowlers' thumbs *stick* in the hole, hanging up the release. Slide-aid creams/powders let the thumb exit cleanly.
- **Buy it if:** your thumb feels like it grabs/sticks on release.

### Bowling gloves
- **What they're for:** consistent hand position and light wrist support; some have padding for comfort. A gentle, non-rigid alternative to a hard wrist brace.
- **Buy it if:** you want a more consistent feel or mild support without committing to a stiff brace.

## Tape & Skin (the consumables — they re-buy these)

> Tapes do two different jobs: **fit-tuning** (make a slightly-loose hole snug) and **skin protection** (stop blisters and tears). Make that distinction on the page — beginners conflate them.

### Thumb / insert tape (fit tape)
- **What it's for:** fine-tuning hole fit. Stick a strip or two inside the thumb hole to take up space as your thumb shrinks (mornings, cold, weight loss) or swells. Lets one drilled hole fit a changing thumb day to day.
- **Two common kinds:** smooth/slick tape (helps the thumb release) and textured/grip tape (helps hold). Bowlers mix them.
- **Buy it if:** you own a drilled ball — basically everyone with their own ball uses thumb tape.

### Finger / skin-protection tape & bandages
- **What it's for:** protecting the skin on fingers and thumb from friction blisters and tears during long sessions.
- **Buy it if:** you bowl multiple games and get sore spots or callus tears.

### Motion / kinesiology tape
- **What it's for:** the wide, stretchy colored tape that supports muscles/joints around the **wrist, elbow, or shoulder** and reduces fatigue on long days. Support, not skin protection.
- **Buy it if:** your wrist/elbow/shoulder fatigues during long blocks of bowling.

## Ball Care

### Ball cleaner
- **What it's for:** lifts lane oil out of the coverstock after bowling. **Oil soaks into reactive covers and kills their hook over time** — regular cleaning keeps the ball reacting like new. The single most impactful care product for a reactive ball.
- **Buy it if:** you own a reactive resin (or particle) ball — yes.

### Microfiber towel / shammy
- **What it's for:** wiping oil off the ball *between shots*, so you throw a clean, consistent ball every frame.
- **Buy it if:** any owned ball — basic kit.

### Resurfacing / abrasive pads (Abralon and similar)
- **What they're for:** changing or restoring the ball's surface texture (duller = more grip/earlier hook; polished = more length/later snap). Usually a shop service, but pads are sold for DIY.
- **Note for copy:** mention the shop offers professional resurfacing — better than DIY for most people.

## Support & Protection

### Wrist supports / braces
- **What they're for:** holding the wrist in a firm, consistent position through the release. Two payoffs: (1) a repeatable release while you're learning, and (2) injury prevention / reducing strain. Rigid metal-and-fabric braces give the most support; soft ones give comfort and a little stability.
- **League-legal:** yes, wrist supports and braces are allowed in USBC-sanctioned league play.
- **Buy it if:** you're learning a consistent release, your wrist tires/cups inconsistently, or you've had wrist soreness.

### Thumb saver / protector
- **What it's for:** a small sleeve or insert that protects the thumb from raw spots and tears and can smooth the release.
- **Buy it if:** your thumb takes a beating over a long session.

## Carry & Storage

> Bag pricing/SKUs live in the separate inventory spec (`02-bags.md`). Here we cover only *what each style is for*, as education copy.

- **Single-ball tote/sling:** lightest, cheapest. For the casual bowler with one ball + shoes.
- **Double / 2-ball roller:** a ball for strikes and a plastic spare ball, plus shoes and accessories. The common league setup.
- **3+ ball roller:** for bowlers carrying an "arsenal" for different lane conditions. Bigger, wheeled, more padding.
- **Shoe bag / accessory pouch:** keeps shoes and consumables organized; protects shoe soles.
- **Copy angle:** match the bag to ball count — "How many balls do you carry?" picks the bag.

## Cross-sell logic for the site (for DesignClaude)

When a shopper views or "gets" an item, suggest the natural companions:

- **Buys a reactive ball →** suggest ball cleaner + microfiber towel + thumb tape (and a fitting).
- **Buys any drilled ball →** suggest thumb tape + a bag sized to their ball count.
- **Buys performance shoes →** suggest a spare slide sole + shoe bag.
- **Beginner learning to hook →** suggest a wrist support + rosin bag.
