## TOP PICK — full A-Z glossary worth mirroring

### IBPSIA Glossary of Bowling Terms
- **URL:** https://ibpsia.com/glossary-of-bowling-terms/
- **Who they are:** International Bowling Pro Shop & Instructors Association — the *pro shop operators' own trade body*. This is the industry-insider source, not a content-marketing blog.
- **Coverage:** ~180–200 terms, A ("Abralon Pad") to W ("Wood Lanes"). Covers ball construction & drilling (cores, coverstock, weight blocks, pins), ball motion/physics (flare, RG, differential, axis rotation), lane conditions (oil patterns, wood vs synthetic), grip/fitting (conventional, fingertip, spans, pitches), and bowler styles (stroker, cranker, spinner, tweener).
- **Gaps:** light on basic *scoring* terms (frames, strikes, spares, splits) — fill those from a general glossary.
- **Why it's the pick:** authoritative (pro-shop trade association), comprehensive, and terminology-focused — exactly the spine for our `kb-05-glossary.md`. Our glossary paraphrases for beginners; this is the source of record for definitions we want to verify against.

## SECONDARY — full education hubs (good for explainer copy)

### bowling.com — Knowledge Hub
- **URL:** https://www.bowling.com/knowledge-hub/
- **Coverage:** large, well-organized retailer education hub with sections for bowling balls (coverstocks, fingertip grip, choosing a ball), shoes (interchangeable soles & heels — excellent, well-structured), tips, and a terms glossary. Written for shoppers, which matches our voice.
- **Specific deep pages worth grabbing:**
  - Coverstock explainer: https://www.bowling.com/knowledge-hub/bowling-balls/simple-guide-to-understanding-bowling-ball-coverstocks
  - Fingertip grip step-by-step: https://www.bowling.com/knowledge-hub/bowling-tips/what-is-a-fingertip-grip-in-bowling-step-by-step-guide
  - Interchangeable soles & heels: https://www.bowling.com/knowledge-hub/bowling-shoes/slide-into-success-understanding-interchangeable-soles-and-heels-for-bowling-shoes
- **Why secondary:** it's a competitor retailer. Great for grounding our explanations; do NOT copy copy verbatim. Rewrite in All Star's voice.

### bowlingball.com — BowlVersity
- **URL:** https://www.bowlingball.com/BowlVersity
- **Coverage:** "hundreds of articles and videos" on ball science, plus an alphabetized "Bowling Terms" index. Deepest single library on ball motion specifically (RG, differential, flare, hook/length potential). Includes the plastic-vs-urethane and ball-basics pieces we grounded `kb-01` on.
- **Notable pages:**
  - Plastic vs Urethane: https://www.bowlingball.com/BowlVersity/plastic-vs-urethane-bowling-balls
  - Ball terminology: https://www.bowlingball.com/BowlVersity/bowling-ball-terminology
- **Why secondary:** also a retailer; same rewrite-don't-copy rule. Best used to fact-check the *numbers* and physics in our spec block.
