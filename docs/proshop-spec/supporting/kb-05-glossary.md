# Beginner's Glossary of Bowling-Shop Terms

> A-Z, written for a shopper, not a physicist. Use as a "Bowling Terms" page and as hover-tooltips on product pages (e.g. a `?` next to "differential" pops the one-line definition).
> Definitions are paraphrased for plain English. For a deeper, manufacturer-grade A-Z glossary to mirror, see `kb-07-source-mirror-notes.md` (IBPSIA is the authoritative source).

---

### A
- **Abralon pad** — an abrasive sanding pad used to change a ball's surface (duller = more grip/earlier hook; smoother = more length).
- **Arc** — a smooth, rounded ball path into the pins, without a sharp late turn. The opposite of an angular "snap."
- **Arsenal** — the set of balls a serious bowler owns for different lane conditions.
- **Asymmetrical core** — a core shape that produces stronger, more angular, more tunable ball motion. (See *Core*.)
- **Axis of rotation** — the imaginary line the ball spins around as it travels. Where it exits your hand is your *PAP* (positive axis point).

### B
- **Backend** — the last third of the lane (closest to the pins), where the ball makes its move toward the pocket.
- **Bowler's tape** — small tape strips placed inside the thumb hole to tune the fit, or on the skin to prevent blisters.
- **Brake heel** — the rubber/leather heel pad on the slide shoe that stops your slide at the foul line.
- **Break point** — the spot down the lane where the ball stops skidding and starts hooking toward the pocket.

### C
- **Conventional grip** — fingers inserted to the second knuckle; the most secure, beginner-friendly drilling. Less hook.
- **Core (weight block)** — the dense shaped block inside the ball that acts as its engine, controlling how it revs and rolls.
- **Coverstock** — the ball's outer shell — the part that touches the lane. Controls friction, which controls hook. The biggest factor in ball reaction.

### D
- **Differential (Diff)** — a spec number for how much the ball can *flare* / hook. Higher = more hook potential and a more angular motion; lower = smoother and milder.
- **Drilling / layout** — how and where the holes are bored relative to the core; aims the ball's reaction to the bowler.

### F
- **Fingertip grip** — fingers inserted only to the first knuckle; produces more revs and hook but needs a precise fit. The committed-bowler grip.
- **Flare (track flare)** — the way a ball shifts its spin axis as it rolls, showing as several oil rings on the ball. More flare = more grip on the lane = more hook potential.
- **Foul line** — the line at the start of the lane; crossing it on your slide is a foul (no pins count).

### H
- **Hook** — the ball curving from its straight path into the pins. The thing most performance gear is designed to create or control.
- **House ball** — the pre-drilled communal balls in the lane racks. One-size conventional fit; the thing your own ball improves on.
- **Hybrid (cover)** — a reactive coverstock blending solid and pearl traits; versatile, common first-hook-ball choice.

### I
- **Insert** — a rubber/silicone grip sleeve placed in a finger hole for comfort and consistent grip; can be swapped/replaced.
- **Interchangeable soles** — swappable Velcro pads on performance shoes that let you tune how much you slide.

### L
- **Lane oil / oil pattern** — lubricant applied to the lane that the ball must travel through. More oil = ball slides more (needs more grip); the *pattern* shapes where the ball hooks.

### M
- **MSRP / street price** — list price vs the real selling price. Bowling balls almost always sell well below MSRP.

### P
- **PAP (Positive Axis Point)** — the exact point the ball rotates off your hand; used by drillers to lay out the ball.
- **Particle (proactive)** — the grippiest coverstock, with embedded particles for maximum traction on heavy oil. Advanced/arsenal gear.
- **Pearl (cover)** — a reactive coverstock that skids longer and snaps harder/later at the backend; cleaner through the fronts.
- **Pin (on the ball)** — a small colored dot marking the top of the core. Its position relative to your hand affects reaction. (Not the thing you knock down.)
- **Pin carry / pin action** — how well the ball knocks pins down and spreads them; stronger covers/cores generally carry better.
- **Pitch** — the angle the finger/thumb holes are drilled at; controls how the hand sits and releases. A key fitting dimension.
- **Plastic / polyester** — the smoothest, straightest, least-hooking coverstock. Beginner balls and dedicated spare balls.
- **Pocket** — the ideal target between the head pin and the next pin (1–3 for righties, 1–2 for lefties) that produces strikes.

### R
- **Reactive resin** — the grippy, high-hook coverstock used in most modern performance balls. Sub-types: solid, pearl, hybrid.
- **Resurfacing** — sanding/polishing a ball's cover to change its reaction; a common pro-shop service.
- **RG (Radius of Gyration)** — a spec number for how early or late the ball revs up. Low RG revs earlier; high RG revs later. (Figure-skater analogy: arms in = fast/low, arms out = slow/high.)
- **Rosin / grip sack** — a powder bag that adds tackiness for sweaty hands.
- **Revs (rev rate)** — how fast the ball is spinning at release; more revs generally means more hook.

### S
- **Slide sole** — the pad on the sliding foot (opposite your throwing hand) that controls how far you glide on your last step.
- **Solid (cover)** — a reactive coverstock with the most surface contact; reads the oil earliest and smoothest. Strongest on heavy oil.
- **Span** — the distance between the thumb hole and finger holes. The most important single fit measurement.
- **Spare** — knocking down the remaining pins on the second ball of a frame; *spare balls* (plastic) are made for this.
- **Symmetrical core** — a core shape that produces smoother, more predictable, more controllable motion. A good default.

### T
- **Thumb saver** — a sleeve/insert protecting the thumb from raw spots and smoothing the release.
- **Track flare** — see *Flare*.
- **Traction sole** — the rubber pad on the push-off foot (same side as your throwing hand) that grips the approach.

### U
- **Urethane** — a coverstock between plastic and reactive: a gentle, predictable, controllable hook. A great first hook ball.
- **USBC** — United States Bowling Congress, the sport's main sanctioning body in the US; sets equipment and league rules.

### W
- **Weight block** — see *Core*.
- **Wrist support / brace** — a device holding the wrist firm for a consistent release and injury prevention; league-legal.
