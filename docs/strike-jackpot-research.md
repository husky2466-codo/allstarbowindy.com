## The two things this research cracked

1. **"Red" almost certainly = a RED HEADPIN game.** Industry-standard mechanic: a colored (red) head pin appears, and a strike thrown while it's up wins/qualifies. It's the most documented use of "red" as a jackpot qualifier. Verified via Brunswick's "Red Head Pin" product module and multiple alleys. **Computer Score (All Star Bowl's actual system) has a configurable Red Pin Game** (confirmed exists on the Bowl-Tech Computer Score/VanTech forum). So their red pin likely fires automatically via Computer Score, while the dollar tiers are tracked by hand on the board.

2. **The most likely 5-tier structure = consecutive-strike tiers**, each with its own rolling pot, red pin as the qualifier on higher tiers. (Double / Turkey / 4-bagger / 5-bagger / 6-bagger ladder.) NOT confirmed at 5 specifically — 3-tier and 6-tier are the documented ones — but 5 is plausible and this is the shape to confirm.

## Variants (so you recognize what he describes)

- **Red Pin / colored headpin** — colored head pin appears (random, via scoring system), strike with it up = prize. THIS is the "red." Brunswick + Computer Score both support it.
- **Mystery frame** — system draws random frames (often via a card deck); strike in all selected frames across 3 games wins. Pot seeds ~$100, $1/entry, rolls over (Greenacres Bowl).
- **Progressive money pot** — buy-in accumulates session over session until won; partial payouts for near-misses (25% for all-but-last strike).
- **Monte Carlo / multi-color pins** — Red/Yellow/Green/Blue pins each pay different instant cash; MUST be called before the shot. Values double each hour.
- **Crazy 8 / pattern** — N strikes in N designated frames; tiered payout (8/8=100%, 7/8=50%). Entry ~$5.
- **Consecutive "X in a row"** — pots keyed to strike streaks; more in a row = bigger pot.

## The 3 plausible 5-tier architectures (confirm which on the call)

- **A — Consecutive-strike tiers (MOST LIKELY):** Double→$5-10, Turkey→$15-25, 4-bagger→$50-75, 5-bagger→$100-150, 6-bagger/front-6→$200-500+. Red pin must be in the run to claim higher tiers. Matches a frame-grid wall board with X marks.
- **B — Average-based tiers:** higher-average bowlers need more strikes (≤170 needs 6, 171-190 needs 10, etc.). Fairness-weighted.
- **C — Buy-in tiers:** pay into different price points, each a harder feat / bigger pot, tracked separately.

## Economics (typical)

- Entry: $1-2/bowler/session (league) up to $5-8 (open night). User floated $5 min buy-in — reasonable.
- Pot: pure accumulation or seeded ~$100; rolls over until won; even split on ties; ~$83 total is normal for a mid-tier neighborhood pot.
- Manual wall-board tracking is the norm at independent alleys; scoring system handles the per-frame red-pin trigger, humans track the cross-session dollar totals.

## Legality (Indiana, brief)

Indiana Code 35-45-5 exempts bona-fide **skill** contests. Alleys frame these as skill (you must throw the strike). The red-pin / specific-frame qualifier is both excitement AND legal scaffolding — the win requires a skill act, not pure chance. Industry runs these openly in Indiana. No IN-specific ruling found.

## CALLBACK CHECKLIST (the questions that pin down their 5 tiers)

**Structure**
1. What qualifies each of the 5 tiers — consecutive strikes (2/3/4/5/6), specific frames, or something else?
2. Does the red pin need to be up for all tiers, or only the top tier(s)?
3. Are the 5 tiers 5 separate running pots, or one total that pays differently by strike count?
4. Does one winner's streak claim all lower tiers too, or does each tier pay independently?
5. Tiers based on bowler average (handicap), or same standard for everyone?

**Economics**
6. Buy-in per bowler per session, and how it splits across tiers?
7. Do bowlers pay into all 5 tiers or choose?
8. Current dollar total in each of the 5 pots right now? (baseline for any digital display)
9. On a win, reset to zero or to a seed?

**Operational**
10. Who manages the board?
11. How is a qualifying run confirmed — staff witness, or Computer Score display?
12. Red pin: automatic via Computer Score, or a physical pin swapped in?
13. League-only or open to all during certain hours?
14. Is the current board the definitive ruleset, or in flux?

**Digital scope (our angle)**
15. Would a real-time live pot-total display (in-alley screen AND website) be useful, or is the hand-drawn board part of the charm they want to keep?
16. Do they track historical wins (who/how much/when) anywhere, or only current totals?
